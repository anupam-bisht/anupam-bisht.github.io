Photoplethysmograph (PPG)

I am currently working on designing and deploying neurotechnologies (specifically brain activity recording and electrical stimulation devices) to study the brains of freely moving rodents. My current interests lie in interfacing hardware with software to better perform neuroscience studies while also using mathematical and simulation tools (inclusive but not limited to machine learning) to gain a better understanding of experiments. My projects often involve collaboration and team work which are critical for the success of projects. In the past, I have extensively studied and performed experiments for steady-state evoked potential (SSVEP) based brain-computer interface systems for humans. I also have interests in pure biology and have been recently been learning to do experiments in a wet lab setting.
